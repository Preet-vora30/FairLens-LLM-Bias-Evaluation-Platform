# FairLens AI — Responsible AI Considerations

## 1. Scope of Measurements

FairLens AI measures statistical associations and behavioral patterns in pretrained language
models. It does not diagnose individual users and does not infer personal attributes from appearance
or identity.

## 2. Construct Validity

- Preference statistics reflect model behavior under specific benchmark protocols.
- They are not conclusive proof of discriminatory intent or real-world harm.
- Reported effect sizes, confidence intervals, and exclusions support uncertainty quantification.

## 3. Ethical Safeguards in the Platform

- Clear labeling of automatic metrics vs. human judgments.
- Transparent metric definitions and scoring methods.
- Reproducible seeds and configuration snapshots.
- Model and dataset attribution.
- Safe custom upload validation (size, extension, structure).
- Local-first storage; no third-party data transmission by default.

## 4. Known Limitations

- Benchmark coverage, dataset representation, language/cultural scope.
- Architecture differences (encoder vs. generative vs. embedding-only evaluation).
- Metric validity boundaries and statistical uncertainty.
- False positives / false negatives possible.
- Distribution shift between benchmark and deployment contexts.
- Mitigation trade-offs (e.g., neutralization may reduce utility).

## 5. Intended Use

Research, education, and pre-deployment auditing. Not a certification or legal compliance tool.

## 6. Reporting

Reports generated by the platform include methodology, results, limitations, references, and
reproducibility details, and avoid overclaiming fairness.
